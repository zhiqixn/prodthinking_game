    Your trip log
    How the pick works
    1. Look at HDB carparks around your destination.
    2. Skip any that are season parking only, have no live data, data older than 15 minutes, or fewer than 10 free car lots. Lots fill up while you drive, so 10 is a safety buffer.
    3. From the rest, pick the shortest walk within 500 m. If none qualify, widen to 1 km, then 1.5 km, and say so.
    4. If it’s raining near your destination (live rain gauges), covered carparks rank ahead of open-air ones within the same distance. Open-air ones are still used if nothing covered qualifies.
    5. The next two become your backups.

    Walking distance is estimated as 1.25 × the straight-line distance. Only HDB carparks have live data for now. Malls and URA carparks aren’t covered yet.
