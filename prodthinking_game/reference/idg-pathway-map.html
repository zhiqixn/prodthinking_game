Pathway map

IDG’s Product Thinking pathway on one page. Product thinking means asking better questions before reaching for solutions.

Learning objectives

  • Write a clear, data-backed problem statement with the 4Cs Framework
  • Tell outputs (features delivered) from outcomes (change achieved)
  • Use root cause analysis, such as the Five Whys, to get past symptoms

Lesson 01 in one line

A solution in disguise names what to build. A problem names who is affected, what goes wrong, and how you know.

Lesson 02 in one line

Ask “why?” from the symptom down, and fix the cause that is within your control and closely connected to the outcome.
