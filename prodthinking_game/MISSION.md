# Mission: Product Thinking (IDG)

## Why
Build the **Product Thinking Game** (`index.html` in this folder): a game that lets players practise the product-thinking habits taught in the Institute of Digital Government's (IDG) Product Thinking pathway. To design good mechanics, I need to understand and use the material myself first.

## Success looks like
- I can tell a real problem statement apart from a solution in disguise, and rewrite one into the other
- I can write a problem statement with IDG's 4Cs Framework
- I can run a Five Whys analysis from a symptom down to a root cause
- I can tell outputs from outcomes and pick metrics that reflect outcomes
- I can turn each IDG module into at least one playable game mechanic

## Constraints
- New to product thinking; start from the basics
- IDG's Product Thinking pathway is the source of truth: https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/
- Lessons are published as shareable artifacts

## Out of scope
- General product-management topics IDG doesn't cover (roadmapping, prioritisation frameworks, pricing)
- How to build AI systems; that belongs to other parts of the AI-301 track
